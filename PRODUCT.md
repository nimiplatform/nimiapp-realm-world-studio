# Realm World Studio

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

This is a derived design brief from the creator's request and `.nimi/spec/realm-world-studio/canonical/`, which remains the sole product authority.

## Platform

web

React renderer in a Desktop-supervised Electron app; the primary usage scene is a desktop creation session.

## Users

World creators who own worlds in Nimi Realm. They want distinctive settings and believable world characters without having to understand schemas or write prompts like an engineer.

## Product Purpose

Make a world easy to start and rewarding to maintain. AI helps develop a creator's premise, expose weak or contradictory settings, and propose characters whose motivations belong in that world. The creator reviews every change.

## Operating Context

Creators move between their Realm worlds and unfinished work, develop a world concept, rules and cast, review AI suggestions, and save canonical world data through Nimi. The user explicitly delegated product and visual decisions and does not want another requirements interview.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Use Nimi app-tools, Kit and SDK built exports. This development iteration has explicit user authorization to use matching local SDK/Kit/native/Runtime builds while their new interfaces remain unpublished. Desktop owns sessions; Realm owns saved worlds and world characters; Runtime owns AI and model configuration. The notebook supports reviewed world creation/replacement, world-character creation/editing and explicit role declarations, alongside AI proposals and protected draft storage. Conflicts preserve work and require another review. World characters are source-setting templates. Canonical save and readback complete the creator task; LocalAgent materialization, trials and downstream readiness are outside this app’s flows and acceptance. No app-owned direct Realm transport or credentials.

## Product Principles

- Start with the creator's idea, in ordinary language.
- Put world setting and character purpose before technical metadata.
- Show AI proposals as editable, reviewable candidates.
- Preserve work when generation, storage or Realm fails.
- Measure success by the actual creation and maintenance journey, not engineering checks alone.

## Brand Commitments

Realm World Studio, a Nimi creator app. Chinese and English interfaces. No invented worlds, activity, users, metrics or AI output presented as real data.
